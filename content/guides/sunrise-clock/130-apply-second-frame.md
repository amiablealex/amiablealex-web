## Apply second half of the casing

![Step13](youtube:WRKXOQ9KPDA)

- Locate the steel rods into each hole, and the single steel rod from the upper casing to go down the center of the concentric shafts.
- Apply carefully so that each rod slots into its hole. Make sure to slot every rod in position carefully at first.
- Once aligned, apply even pressure all the way around to gradually close the casings together.

### Warning: 
Do not press so far as to clamp the casing onto the gears, otherwise the mechanism will lock, prevent the gears from turning and the clock will not work. There should be free space for the gears to spin without touching the casing.
