## Take one frame half and follow these steps:
![Step02](youtube:-6B5ES5ewag)

- Run a 1.5mm drill bit into each hole by hand. Make sure to keep the drill pointed directly into the hole, at no angle.
- To make this easier/faster, after using the drill piece by hand, next you can use an electric drill - just be careful not to drill all the way through the frame - these are not through holes.

A clean pass drilling out the holes with the 1.5mm drill bit should be enough to push the steel rods into place - make sure you can press the steel rod all the way into the hole. If using a drill you should be able to feel it when the drill bit hits the ‘floor’ of the hole - don't drill out any further.
The steel rods should maintain their position with some resistance in the hole, and not fall out easily.

# Be careful:
The tall fragile poles are susceptible to break (my bad design). If they do break, the whole height of the pole is not essential, so the frame may not be wasted just yet. The purpose of the holes in the cage is for the alignment of the steel rods, which is critical. Once the gears are assembled into place onto the rods the gears will align themselves vertically and the height of the pole may not necessarily matter.
