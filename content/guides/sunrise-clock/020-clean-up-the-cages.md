title: Clean up the frames
summary: "Similar to the previous step, we need to clean up the holes for the rods in both frame pieces. Unlike the holes for the rotating parts, the goal for the frames is NOT resistance-free. The goal here is for the steel rods to slot in place with some resistance and hold themselves there."
---

![This is an example youtube embed](youtube:dQw4w9WgXcQ)

## Take one frame half and follow these steps: 
1. Run a 1.5mm drill bit into each hole by hand. Make sure to keep the drill pointed directly into the hole, at no angle. To make this easier you can use an electric drill - just be careful not to drill all the way through the frame - these are not through holes.

A clean pass drilling out the holes with the 1.5mm drill bit should be enough to assemble the steel rods into place. If using a drill you should be able to feel it when the drill bit hits the ‘floor’ of the hole - don't drill out any further.

## BE CAREFUL:
The tall fragile poles are susceptible to break (my bad design). If they do break, the whole height of the pole is not essential, so the frame may not be wasted just yet. The purpose of the holes in the cage is for the alignment of the steel rods, which is critical. Once the gears are assembled into place onto the rods the gears will align themselves vertically and the height of the pole may not necessarily matter.
