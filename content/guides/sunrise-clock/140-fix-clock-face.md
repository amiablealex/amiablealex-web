## Fix the clock face to the casing

![Step14](youtube:MgMOAJN-ax8)

- Carefully apply glue to the underside of the face contact point, around the outside and not near the center hole. Be careful not to get glue in the center of the hole because we don't want it to stick to the brass shaft when we press the face into place.
- Press the face into place, aligned with one of the double-spokes vertically upwards, hold gentle pressure for 30 seconds. 
- Leave to dry for ~5 to 10 minutes
- Apply small amount of glue to underside of the gauge - don't use too much as it will ooze out when we press it.
- Press the gauge onto place, with a check mark pointing vertically upwards. Hold with constant gentle pressure for 30 seconds.
