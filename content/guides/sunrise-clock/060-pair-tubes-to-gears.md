## Push brass rods into their gears

![Step06](youtube:-yTUq0W2sME)

- Push brass rods into their respective gears. Just push in to the right position to get started
- Tap with a hammer to push the tube fully into the gear
- Make sure brass rod is flush with the far side of the gear
