## Put the Cam Assembly together

![Step07](youtube:K6TUqPuisro)

Be careful to get orientation right:
- Smallest of the two cams sandwiched in the middle, largest cam on the outside.
- Orientation matters: look at the bulges on each cam, and the thickness of each 'lip' edge. The thickest edges should be facing the outside, and the thinnest edges of each cam touching each other.
- Both cam spokes should be aligned parallel to each other.
