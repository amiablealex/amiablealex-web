## Assemble the gear-sector-spring pivot assemblies

![Step09](youtube:NjnoPBW88qI)

- Apply a hairspring to each of the pins, with the locking piece of the hairspring facing down
- Take the pin-spring assembly and apply the sector piece on top of the hairspring
- Make sure the largest sector goes with the slightly taller pin
- Apply each the sector so that the hairspring locking pin is oriented according to the pictures below
