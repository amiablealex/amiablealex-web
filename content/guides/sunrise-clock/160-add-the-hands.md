## Add the hour indicator
- Press fit the hour hand onto the hour shaft (largest of the three brass tubes). It can be fixed on in any position. The hour mechanism is still loose so the hand should rotate freely.

## Add the Sunrise and Sunset indicators

- Look up the sunrise & sunset times (in GMT) for the winter solstice at your location. 
- Position the sunset indicator pointing to the correct time on the clock gauge and press onto the shaft, trying to keep the indicator pointing to the correct direction as best you can.
- Then do the same for the sunrise indicator onto the smallest brass tube - position the indicator in the correct direction pointing to the winter solstice sunrise time (GMT), and press it onto the smallest brass shaft.

![step16 finished](step16-finished.png)

### Warning:
This is the part of the v1 design guide that I am least happy about and will be addressed as part of a v2 redesign. It's really difficult to keep the hands pointing in the right direction when you're pressing them onto the shafts, and defeats the whole point of printing an accurate cam profile.
For now - it works - you can adjust the position of the hands slightly after you've pressed them on by gripping the respective shaft with a pair of pliers and forcing the hand to move. But it's nasty.
