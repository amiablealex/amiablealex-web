## Push the drive gear onto the clock mechanism's hour shaft

![Step08](youtube:t3e6I_Hkdbw)

- Push-fit the driving gear onto the clock mechanism's shaft for the hour hand (one full rotation every 12 hours, the outer-most thickest shaft on the mechanism).
- The design suits a specific type of clock mechanism bought from amazon [UTS IEC R6 High Torque Quartz Clock Mechanism](https://amzn.eu/d/03vyiZzt).
- Different mechanism will need adjustment to the drive gear center hole so that it fits the shaft.
