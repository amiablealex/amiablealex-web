## Cut the brass tubes

![Step05](youtube:nk67uAN1udk)

- Measure and mark the brass rods: 8mm tube to 35mm length, 6mm tube to 47mm length, and 4mm tube to 62mm length
- Cut using a saw (can be quite difficult). Start by making a feint groove with the saw, then eventually the first cut into the brass, and then all the way through. Can bend the final bit to ‘break off’.
- Use coarse sandpaper to sand the rough end of the brass rods.

![Step05 final](step5-final.png)

### Be careful:
Saw is sharp
