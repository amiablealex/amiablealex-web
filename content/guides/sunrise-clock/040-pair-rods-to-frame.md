## Assemble the steel rods into the frames

![Step04](youtube:Lgb1Jxg6rW8)

- Push **8x 40mm** steel rods and **1x 15mm** steel rod into the main frame (the one with the square clock mechanism holder). The short rod goes in the position nearest the square clock mechanism holder. Be careful not to break the tall towers.
- After positioning the rods, go around each one tapping with a hammer to make sure all are pushed into the bottom of their hole properly. You’ll know the rod is in all the way because the hammer taps will sound different (more solid, louder).
- Push **the remaining 40mm** steel rod into the other half of the frame (one position only - nearest the two poles that are close together).
- Tap with hammer so the rod goes in all the way.
