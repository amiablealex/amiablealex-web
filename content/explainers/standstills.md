title: The moon's eighteen-year swing
topic: The Moon

summary: "Every month the moon swings north and south of the equator, but how far it swings changes over 18.6 years: out to 28.6°, further than the sun ever goes, then in to 18.3°. How Earth's tilt and the moon's own take turns to add and cancel, why the turning points are called standstills, and the Colorado great house whose rebuilding dates fall in standstill years."
